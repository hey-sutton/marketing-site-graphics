# Beverage distributors graphics: generator and tests

Source for `graphics/beverage-distributors/card-1.js` to `card-4.js`, Graphic Key `beverage-distributors`. The manifest lists `"beverage-distributors": [1, 2, 3, 4]` and the version is bumped to 1.52.0 in the same pull request. The release tag, the loader's `BASE`, the CMS Graphic Key and the `usp-4---image` fallback photo are left to the release owner.

* **Frame:** copied from `tools/distilleries` (the reference build): the shared Watch and Try it frame (`tools/electronics/frame.py`, 716 by 640 window, Studio badge and popover, Replay, ghost cursor, ripple, captions, visibility observer, aria live region, reduced motion) with the app shell (`shell=True`) and the phone layout (`phone=True`). `build.py` adds, for this page only:
  * modules for a beverage distributor in the (hidden) shell: Inventory, Purchasing, Warehouse, Traceability, Sales, Delivery; company "Saltline Beverage Co";
  * "Get help: From the Sutton team" in the Studio popover (no plan wording);
  * no step arrows, no status pill and no module nav at any width (November 24, 2026 rules);
  * the phone stage: a 10 px strip of photo under the window, 40 px top bar controls, an icon only Studio chip under 430 px, and a short detail list (`xw()`) so short screens have no empty band;
  * read only inputs (`rof()`, `RO_CSS`): every field is prefilled with the walkthrough's value, `readonly`, `tabindex="-1"`, gray and ignores the pointer. No typing, no typing animation, no sample buttons and no error messages. The reader only clicks;
  * `prune()` drops scaffold CSS a card never uses, which keeps every card between 60 and 65 KB.
* **Build:** `python3 tools/beverage-distributors/build.py` rebuilds all four, or pass card numbers. It first imports `numbers_check.py`, which asserts every sum in the brief (the 180 + 90 + 30 stock split, 6.0, 20.0, 12.0 and 30.0 days, 760, 780, 250 short, 2,850.00, lots 180 + 420 + 600 = 1,200, the 180 + 320 = 500 pick, 960 = 40 x 24, 780 + 180 = 960, 1,690.00, 60.00, 1,750.00, boards 23 and 24).
* **Card 1: Available stock and peak season planning.** Four number tiles above a planning table with coverage bars. Background 1. Start: the planning table of four SKUs (baseline) and four empty tiles. Pick Citrus Seltzer 12 pack in the table: 300 in stock, 150 expected, 700 committed over 14 days, 50 a day, 6.0 days. Open In stock: warehouse 180, cooler 90, 3PL 30 = 300. Check the 14 day window: the row's coverage bar turns amber, 300 + 150 less 700 = 250 short. Draft PO-2214 to Kestrel Springs Beverage: 250 at $11.40 = $2,850.00. Other rows: Harbor Lager 20.0 days, Orchard Juice 12.0, Cold Brew RTD 30.0. Phone: tiles as a 2 by 2 grid, a three column table (SKU, days, status), the stock split, the shortage and the order each as their own screen.
* **Card 2: Lot dates and oldest first picking.** Horizontal bars of days left per lot with a 30 day threshold line. Background 2. Start: the SKU list only. Open Harbor Lager 24 pack: as of Nov 16, HL-2611-A best by Dec 14 (28 days, 180), B Jan 25 (70 days, 420), C Mar 8 (112 days, 600), 1,200 cases (card 1's stock). Start the pick for SO-5126 (read only, Northgate Grocers, 500 cases): 180 from A, then 320 from B. Check lot A: 28 days is under the 30 day threshold, 180 cases at risk. Offer lot A to Dockside Taproom: lot A drops to 0.
  * **Interpretation:** the brief says the pick takes 180 from A and also that lot A's 180 cases go to Dockside Taproom. To keep both, the 500 case order is Northgate Grocers', which needs 30 days on arrival (shelf life rules by account are on screen), so lot A is flagged for that order and offered to Dockside Taproom, which takes 14 days. The Northgate pick then moves to lots B and C (420 + 80 = 500) and 520 cases are left. Those two derived numbers are asserted too.
  * Phone: three lot bars stacked with days left at the right, the pick as its own screen.
* **Card 3: Recall lookups from lot to account.** Left to right trace: supplier lot, received, shipped and on hand, accounts. Background 3. Start: the supplier lot field (read only BRW-4471) and an empty prompt. Trace at about 1.3 s after the card is in view (`ENGINE_PATCH`), full trace by about 2 s: Harbor Brewing lot BRW-4471, 960 received (40 pallets of 24), 780 shipped to Dockside Taproom 120, Greenline Market 240, Pier 9 Bar 60, Northgate Grocers 300, Elm Street Liquor 60, and 180 on hand in cooler bin C-04. Open the shipped cases: the five accounts with order, delivery date (Nov 6 to 12) and cases. Place hold H-0311 on the 180. Download the list (recall-BRW-4471.csv). Phone: the flow as a vertical list.
* **Card 4: Orders, deposits and delivery status.** Order form with a deposit line above a four column status board. Background 4. Start: the account field (read only Pier 9 Bar), an empty order form and the board at Open 5, Picked 4, Loaded 3, Delivered 11 (23). Use the account: its price list applies to the SKU chips. Tap the three SKUs (one step, three clicks, the first two marked `data-pick`): Harbor Lager 40 at $24.50 = $980.00 (760 available covers 40), Cold Brew RTD 20 at $19.00 = $380.00 (780 covers 20), half barrel keg 2 at $165.00 = $330.00, and the keg deposit as its own line, 2 at $30.00 = $60.00. Goods $1,690.00 + $60.00 = $1,750.00. Create SO-5131: Open 6 (24). Move it to delivered: Open 5, Picked 4, Loaded 3, Delivered 12 (24). Phone: lines with price only, the deposit line below, the board as four tiles.
* **Studio chips:** the brief lists two Studio sections; the second (coverage window, near date threshold, supplier contact field, deposit type and so on) matches these cards and is used. The first set was a distilleries leftover.
* **Brief items replaced by the November 24, 2026 rules:** no step arrows (the brief asked for them), and no typing or sample buttons on card 3 (the lot field is prefilled and read only, one click traces it).
* **Tests:** `test_card1.py` to `test_card4.py` run the shared harness (`tools/electronics/testlib.py`) through `bd_tests.py` (adapted from `distilleries_tests.py`, plus `readonly_checks()`, a mouse only walk and `click_step()` for card 4's three tap step). Each asserts the brief's numbers in Python, then checks in the browser: Watch and Try it, the take over, every caption and number, a wrong click, Replay, the toggle, the hidden arrows and pill, the Studio popover, reduced motion, the empty start screen with its read only fields, start within 1.5 s and the first result by 4 s, layout at 1300, 1024, 768 and 390 px (right and bottom edge hidden, square corner, no empty band), a navigation walk at 1300, 768, 480, 390 and 340 px, the phone stage rules, and a mouse only walk at 604 and 358 px. The text scan bans dashes as punctuation, "Digit", "recipe", plan and tier wording, market size words, concept or illustrative tags, certification words and real breweries, beverage brands, distributors, retailers and carriers.
* `shots.py N [card widths]` steps one card through Try it and saves a screenshot per step.

Run with `pip install playwright pillow`. Set `PW_CHROME` to a Chromium binary already on the machine. Screenshots go to `GFX_SHOTS` (default `/tmp/bd_shots`).

The backgrounds are copies of `graphics/brewery/bg-1.jpg` to `bg-4.jpg`, one per card (604 by 540, progressive JPEG, about 50 KB). All names, quantities, dates and prices are invented sample data for illustration; supplier and account names are invented.
