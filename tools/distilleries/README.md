# Distilleries graphics: generator and tests

Source for `graphics/distilleries/card-1.js` to `card-4.js`, page /industries/distilleries, Graphic Key `distilleries`. The manifest lists `"distilleries": [1, 2, 3, 4]`. The version bump, the release tag, the loader's `BASE` and the CMS Graphic Key are left to the release owner.

* **Frame:** the shared Watch and Try it frame (`tools/electronics/frame.py`, the Brewery scaffold at 716 by 640: shadow DOM host, Studio badge and popover, Replay, hint pill, ghost cursor, captions, step arrows, ripple hotspots, visibility observer, aria live region, reduced motion) with the app shell (`shell=True`) and the phone layout (`phone=True`), like Footwear. `build.py` adds, for distilleries only:
  * distillery modules in the shell: Production, Warehouse, Inventory, Traceability, Sales, Purchasing;
  * "Get help: From the Sutton team" in the Studio popover (no plan wording);
  * no step arrows and no status pill (Watching, Your turn, Done) at any width (November 24, 2026); the Watch and Try it toggle, Replay and the prompts inside the window stay;
  * on the phone stage (card under 520 px): a 10 px strip of photo under the window, 40 px top bar controls, and an icon only Studio chip under 430 px (its name stays for screen readers);
  * a short detail list on phone stages 430 px and wider (`xw()`), so the taller stage has no empty band;
  * card 3 starts sooner (`ENGINE_PATCH`), so the lot is typed about 1.3 s after the card is in view;
  * scaffold CSS rules whose classes a card never uses are dropped from that card's file (`prune()`), which keeps every card at or under 65 KB.
* **Build:** `python3 tools/distilleries/build.py` rebuilds all four, or pass card numbers. It first imports `numbers_check.py`, which asserts every sum in the brief (mash bill totals, 346.00, 2,000.00, 4.00 per liter, 1.80, 3.25, 19.50, 20.35, 692.00, 511.0, 110, 3.4%, 80 + 70 = 150, 560, 400 + 100 + 60, 1,200, 300 short, 276.00).
* **Card 1: Mash bills, blends and cost per bottle.** BOM tree with a cost panel. Start: an empty SKU search. Pick Barrel Rye 750 ml (sample, or type it and press Enter): the bottle costs $3.25 (blend 1.80 + glass 0.92 + closure 0.18 + label 0.09 + back label 0.04 + labor 0.22), case of 6 $19.50 + carton 0.85 = $20.35. Open the blend: 450 ml distillate + 300 ml water, distillate share 450 ml at $4.00 per L = $1.80. Open the distillate: batch DS-0412, 500 L, grain $346.00 (1,400 lb corn at 0.14, 400 lb rye at 0.22, 200 lb malted barley at 0.31) + yeast and enzymes $24.00 + labor, energy and still time $1,630.00 = $2,000.00, $4.00 per L. Scale the mash to 4,000 lb: 2,800, 800 and 400 lb, grain $692.00, 1,000 L, still $4.00 per L. Mash bill v3 is current; v2 and v1 are listed with their batches. Phone: three levels of the tree with the cost at the right, the scale control as two buttons.
* **Card 2: Barrel records and rickhouse locations.** Rickhouse grid with a barrel record panel. Start: Rickhouse B, six rows of five levels by ten positions, every barrel in place, nothing selected. Open row 4 (50 barrels of lot DS-0412, which holds 120 across rows 4 to 6), open barrel 4-3-09 (filled Mar 14, 2022 from batch DS-0412, 4 yr 8 mo, fill 53.0 L, gauge 51.2 L), compare gauge with fill (1.8 L, 3.4%), select level 3 (gauges 51.2, 51.0, 50.8, 51.4, 51.1, 50.9, 51.3, 51.0, 51.2, 51.1 = 511.0 L), dump to blend tank BT-2: the lot drops to 110 and BT-2 holds 511.0 L. Phone: a 6 by 5 grid (positions 5 to 10) and the barrel record as its own screen.
* **Card 3: Lot tracing and recall lookups.** Left to right flow of lots, batches, bottling runs and accounts. Start: an empty lot field. BT-2209 (juniper) is typed about 1.3 s in: gin batch GN-118, run A BR-0921 80 cases (accounts 60, 12, 8), run B BR-0928 70 cases (accounts 30, 40), 150 cases. Follow run B back to its five source lots, open the recall lookup (five accounts, 150 cases), open Harbor Wine and Spirits (60 cases on SO-4471). Phone: the flow as a vertical list.
* **Card 4: Release allocation and dry goods planning.** Four number tiles above a channel split table. Start: Single Barrel Bourbon 750 ml, fall release, baseline tiles 900, 300, 640, 560 and an empty split table. Open Available (900 + 300 less 640 = 560), split 560 (distributors 400, tasting room 100, online 60, typed fields with a live total), commit (committed 1,200, available 0), open the bottle shortage for run BR-0940 (1,800 needed, 1,500 on hand, 300 short), raise PO-3412 for 300 bottles at $0.92 = $276.00. Phone: tiles as a 2 by 2 grid, the split table with three columns, the shortage as its own screen.
* **Tests:** `test_card1.py` to `test_card4.py` run the shared harness (`tools/electronics/testlib.py`) through `distilleries_tests.py` (adapted from Footwear's, with the variety check from `tools/footwear`). Each asserts the brief's numbers in Python, then checks in the browser: Watch and Try it, the take over, every caption and number, a wrong click, invalid input, Replay, the toggle, the hidden arrows and pill, the Studio popover, reduced motion, the empty start screen, start within 1.5 s and the first result by 4 s, typing with Enter (cards 1 and 3) and typing into the split (card 4), layout at 1300, 1024, 768 and 390 px (right and bottom edge hidden, square corner, no empty band), a navigation walk at 1300, 768, 480, 390 and 340 px, and on the phone stage: shape, a slim photo strip under the window, 40 px controls, one line captions, nothing cut off. The text scan bans dashes as punctuation, "Digit", "recipe", plan and tier wording, market size words, concept or illustrative tags and real distillery, spirits brand, distributor and retailer names.
* `shots.py N [card widths]` steps one card through Try it and saves a screenshot per step.

Run with `pip install playwright pillow`. Set `PW_CHROME` to a Chromium binary already on the machine. Screenshots go to `GFX_SHOTS` (default `/tmp/distilleries_shots`).

The backgrounds are copies of `graphics/brewery/bg-1.jpg` to `bg-4.jpg`, one per card (604 by 540, progressive JPEG, about 50 KB). All names, quantities and readings are invented sample data.
